1. The Apex Structure: Why High-Growth Startups File as Delaware C-Corporations

When venture capitalists, angel investors, or private equity firms look to write a check, they look for one specific entity structure: The Delaware C-Corporation. If you are operating as an LLC or a C-Corp in another state, you are introducing friction into the investment process.

The Strategic Advantages:

  • Investor Preference: VCs strongly prefer Delaware C-Corps due to the predictability of the Delaware Court of Chancery, which specializes strictly in corporate law.
  • Tax Advantages (QSBS): Under Section 1202 of the IRC, founders and early investors in Qualified Small Business Stock (QSBS) can potentially exclude up to 100% of capital gains taxes upon selling their shares—a benefit exclusive to C-Corps.
  • Equity Distribution: Issuing stock options, restricted stock units (RSUs), and managing an Employee Stock Ownership Plan (ESOP) is exponentially cleaner and standardized in a C-Corp compared to the complex profit-interest modeling required in an LLC.

The Drawbacks of DIY Formation:

Attempting to restructure or form a Delaware C-Corp independently often leads to catastrophic cap table errors, missing 83(b) elections (resulting in massive unexpected tax bills for founders), and non-compliant bylaws. While DIY platforms offer low upfront costs, the legal cleanup required before a Series A funding round can cost tens of thousands of dollars.

2. Establishing Corporate Identity: DUNS Numbers & Crunchbase

What is a DUNS Number?

A Data Universal Numbering System (DUNS) number is a unique nine-digit identifier issued by Dun & Bradstreet. It acts as your company's "Social Security Number" for business credit.

  • Why you need it: You cannot secure Apple Developer accounts, apply for federal government contracts, or establish a robust corporate credit profile (independent of your personal credit) without one.
  • V&K DUNS Tracker: V&K Partners monitors your DUNS registry health, ensuring your corporate telemetry matches your credit profile perfectly, keeping you highly fundable and mitigating vendor risk flags.

What is Crunchbase?

Crunchbase is the premier master database for discovering industry trends, investments, and corporate data. A verified, well-maintained Crunchbase profile acts as a massive trust signal to the ecosystem.

  • The Benefit: It provides inbound visibility to venture capitalists, tech journalists, and enterprise procurement teams conducting due diligence. A blank or non-existent Crunchbase profile signals a lack of market maturity.
